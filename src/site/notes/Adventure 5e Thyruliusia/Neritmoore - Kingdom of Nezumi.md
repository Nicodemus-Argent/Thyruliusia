### **FROM ME**
Ratfolk, Nezumi, come from the small valley kingdom of Neritmoore. Here their predominant crops are rice and corn. They do not keep many animals domesticated for food. They often hunt birds like pheasant, turkey, and qauil in the woods.
Their greatest crafting capability is for wood, and they buy most of their metal work from the dwarves who live in the northern most Tor. Their pottery is not the greatest, but they make due. The few that adventure out are often mistaken as were-rats until they prove that they are much more civilized.
