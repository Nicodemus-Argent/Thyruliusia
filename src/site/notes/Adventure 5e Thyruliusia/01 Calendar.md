# The Calendar of Ladriande

A year consists of fourteen months, each containing
five weeks of seven days.

## **The Fourteen Months of the Dragon Calendar**

|Month|Proposed name|Meaning / character|
|---|---|---|
|1|**Wyrmrest**|The long repose. A Dragon's time is not measured by how often it wakes.|
|2|**Hoardwake**|The awakening of interest in one's accumulated wealth and possessions.|
|3|**Goldtide**|A period associated with wealth, acquisition, and prosperity.|
|4|**Sovereign**|The exercise of authority. A reminder that Dragons rule.|
|5|**Scaleheart**|The enduring nature of Dragonkind, across all lineages.|
|6|**Emberfall**|The passing of old things and the beginning of new fires.|
|7|**Eyrie**|A time of elevation, distance, and looking down upon lesser concerns.|
|8|**Conclave**|The gathering of Dragons and the settling of great matters.|
|9|**Deepvault**|The preservation of treasures, knowledge, and secrets.|
|10|**Fangwake**|A reminder that even sleeping powers can become dangerous.|
|11|**Dominion**|The maintenance and expansion of one's influence.|
|12|**Longshadow**|The passage of time as experienced by beings who outlive generations.|
|13|**Tribute**|What lesser beings provide to sustain the great.|
|14|**Pittance**|A sardonic reference to the tiny portions of time required by mortal servants.|


### The Stars of the Week
## **The Twelve Great Stars**

|Star|What people see|
|---|---|
|**Aurelion, the Crown**|An exceptionally bright golden-white star, traditionally associated with authority and rulership.|
|**Vesper, the Evening Eye**|A brilliant star that appears particularly prominent after sunset.|
|**Thornwake**|A sharp, reddish star associated with conflict, endurance, and hardship.|
|**The Pale Lantern**|A steady, soft-white star, often used by travelers for navigation.|
|**Mournstar**|A dim blue star associated with death, remembrance, and the departed.|
|**The Wanderer**|A star whose position appears to shift noticeably over long periods.|
|**Kharad, the Furnace**|A powerful orange-red star, associated with fire, creation, and destruction.|
|**The Twin Lights**|Two close stars that appear almost inseparable, traditionally associated with companionship and duality.|
|**The Serpent's Head**|A greenish-white star within a distinctive winding arrangement of smaller stars.|
|**The Broken Spear**|A bright star with a long trail of fainter stars extending from it.|
|**The Deep Crown**|A remote, faintly violet star, difficult to see except on clear nights.|
|**The Last Watch**|A brilliant star that dominates the sky during a particular part of the year.|

These are deliberately common translated names rather than the original names the Dragon Empire used. Of these, 7 have named the days of the week.
