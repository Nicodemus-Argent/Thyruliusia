# Deldruke Kingdom
By virtue of surface dwelling Elves of Naven Forest  [[Naven Forest\|Naven Forest]], this nation is more commonly called Giledzar, after the surface level city of [[Adventure 5e Thyruliusia/Nations/Deldruke Kingdom - Nation/Giledzar Capital\|Giledzar Capital]] which is the *trade* capital of the nation. This is the massive dwarven nation set under the mountains that occupy tens of thousands of square miles of the world. Though predominately underground in dozens of cities and literally millions of caverns, the nation also holds the surrounding lands under their power and protection.

Deeper into the mountains, high above where the Dwarves emerge, there are two separate states of Giants who occupy the land as well. Though Dwarves and Giants are natural enemies, they have come to a pragmatic understanding and tentative truce in the last 140 years.
