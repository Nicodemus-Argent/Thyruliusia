## Overview
A large, circular city spanning a river. Two full levels, circles, of city walls and sprawling outskirts. Surrounded by miles of farmland.

# Notable Locations
### The Guildhall
Located in Court Farthing is an impressive timber and brick building, decorated with finely carved gargoyles. It contains a large meeting hall and several smaller rooms, and is shared amongst several local merchant guilds.

### A Few NPCs
- Sybel: Female Human Aristocrat, Neutral. Sybel is rugged in appearance, with grey hair and brown eyes. She wears fine raiment and jewelry. Sybel was magically imprisoned for a hundred years.
- Raffolk: Male Human Fighter, Good. Raffolk is youthful in appearance, with black hair and blue eyes. He wears banded mail and wields a long sword and shield. Raffolk is imaginative but abrasive.
