# Augustus Velindiel
Sixth of HIs Name.
Sovereign Lord of the Nation of Ladriande. Inducted in the 10th month of 461, 2 months after the death of his father Nathaniel Velindiel, 3rd of His Name.

Augustus is in his mid 30s. He is a well educated Noble and a skilled swordsman. He has already proven capable, maintaining trade relations with the Dwarven nation of Giledzar  [[Adventure 5e Thyruliusia/Nations/Deldruke Kingdom - Nation/Giledzar Capital\|Giledzar Capital]] to the North, keeping military hostilities with the Shiva Empire to the NE, and otherwise keeping the nation out of political complications that plague other parts of the world.
