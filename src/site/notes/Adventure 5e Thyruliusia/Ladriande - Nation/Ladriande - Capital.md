# Nation

## Capital Ladriande

### Description
The city

### Demographics
Large City, population apx 24,000
60% Human
15% Elven
5% Dwarven
20% Beastfolk and others


### Government
Ladriande is Ruled by Lord Velindiel (Male Human Noble)

### Notable Places:

- The Guildhall: Located in Court Farthing is an impressive timber and brick building, decorated with finely carved gargoyles. It contains a large meeting hall and several smaller rooms, and is shared amongst several local merchant guilds.

### A Few NPCs
- Sybel: Female Human Aristocrat, Neutral. Sybel is rugged in appearance, with grey hair and brown eyes. She wears fine raiment and jewelry. Sybel was magically imprisoned for a hundred years.
- Raffolk: Male Human Fighter, Good. Raffolk is youthful in appearance, with black hair and blue eyes. He wears banded mail and wields a long sword and shield. Raffolk is imaginative but abrasive.
