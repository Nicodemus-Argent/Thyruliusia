The Town of Briarlaize is second in size only to the capital of Ladriande.  Sitting at the crossroads of the Grand East Road and the winding Silvervein River, Briarlaize is the undisputed breadbasket of the realm. Because it lies a mere thirty miles from the capital, the town operates as a massive, bustling supply depot where the harvests of a hundred surrounding farms are sorted, taxed, and loaded onto river barges or heavy wagon trains.

While humans make up the majority of the landowning families, Briarlaize is a vibrant melting pot. Ratfolk engineers maintain the massive, water-powered grain mills along the riverbanks, while tanuki merchants run the sprawling open-air markets and roadside taverns. Kitsune scribes and halfling factors work side-by-side in the counting houses, ensuring the capital’s 24,000 citizens never go hungry. Despite its pastoral reputation, Oakhaven is fiercely pragmatic and surprisingly wealthy, guarded by a well-funded militia that treats bandits and crop-blights with equal hostility.
