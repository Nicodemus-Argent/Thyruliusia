# Ladriande - Nation

Ruler: Lord Velindiel

Size: 25 hex X 14 hex, approximately 95,000 square miles.


Capital: Ladriande City

The national boarders include Oceans to the NE and SW. To the NW is the Desert of Desolation and the SE the Manjan Waste, which is the Eastern most national boarder. The North boarder is the Ayachoozcolla Jungle, which only includes a little into the nation. The access to the ocean of the South is flanked by a wide Forest with only a narrow corridor to reach the waters.

The NE ocean is actually an enormous fresh water lake, it offers no ocean access, but is important for resources and trade.
The SW ocean is a very large bay, one can access the open sea, but some of the passes are treacherous and the territory is often contested by various states.

Ladriande is largely self sufficient. The farmland is abundant, the timber is valuable, but access to the area involves harsh sea or desert crossings, so the area is largely ignored.
The farming communities, spread far and wide, usually have hamlets that are not dropped into the map. Any given 8 mile hex can hold as many as 150 farming families. Any hex can easily have a very small collective center with its own individual identity as a community.
