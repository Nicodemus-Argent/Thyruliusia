The planar alignment engine, the rotational matrix by which The Outlands interacts with the world.

If you survive going into the deep frozen polar caps you can literally walk onto The Outlands.
When the ocean tide is high enough at certain parts of the globe, your ship can literally be dropped out onto there, so there are certain parts of the ocean that are avoided at all cost during the proper tides and some fools who chance it.

There are 16 sections of the outlands, fan blades around a central axis, the enormous, impossibly thin mountain known as The Spire.
Picture now if The Outlands were a gear with 16 teeth, and Thyruliusia a larger gear with 23 teeth. As the two turn, connected, they make contact in a predictable pattern. One area of The Outlands in contact with one of 23 sections of Thyruliusia. When The Outlands have completed one full rotation, Thyruliusia has not. So, geographically, The Outlands are slowly rolling over the globe and different parts touch at different times.

Great intellects study the speed and intensity of the contact and can track what area will experience fortune or despair. Some communities and nations pay very close attention, particularly those close to the contact areas. Some do not care at all and take what fate hands them.
