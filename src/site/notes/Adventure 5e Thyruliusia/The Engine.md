The planar alignment engine, the rotational matrix by which The Outlands interacts with the world.

If you survive going into the deep frozen polar caps you can literally walk onto The Outlands.
When the ocean tide is high enough at certain parts of the globe, your ship can literally be dropped out onto there, so there are certain parts of the ocean that are avoided at all cost during the proper tides and some fools who chance it.
