**Long Term Goal:** Reclaims the Relics forged for himself, as well as the other four Stoneborn. 

**Long Term Dream:** Understand his origin and creation intimately enough to carve another of his kind, himself. 

**Short Term Goal:** Better understand and rediscover the now lost and reclusive Rockseer elves.

## Goals of Eymriella

**SHORT TERM**
* Send money home. Earn enough from adventuring and healing work to regularly send part of her income to her parents and younger siblings.
* Expand her medical knowledge. Collect books, recipes, herbal remedies, and information about magical illnesses. She keeps everything she learns in a personal medical journal.
* Understand Invisible Lifeline. Discover where her unusual healing gift came from, why she possesses it, and what its true limits are.
* Build a traveling healer’s kit. Gather better medicines, rare herbs, bandages, potions, and equipment so she can treat injuries without relying entirely on magic.
* Protect her adventuring party. Gradually develop jeong with her companions until she begins viewing them as another family.
* Find rare medicinal plants. Whenever the party travels somewhere new, she searches for plants she has never encountered and records their properties.
* Become more confident as a healer. She knows a great deal from books, but now she must learn how to make difficult decisions when real lives depend upon her.
* Write home frequently. No matter how busy adventuring becomes, she refuses to let her family think she has forgotten them.

**LONG TERM**
Eymriella’s Long-Term Goals

* Give her parents the life they deserve. Her greatest goal is to earn enough money to buy them a comfortable home with plenty of food, warmth, and land where they can grow old without working themselves to exhaustion.
* Educate her siblings. She wants every sibling—especially the younger ones—to have access to education and opportunities she never had.
* Create a free healing clinic. Eventually, Eymriella wants to establish a clinic where poor families can receive treatment regardless of whether they can afford it.
* Build a library beside the clinic. She dreams of collecting medical, botanical, agricultural, historical, and magical knowledge and making it available to ordinary people. Her philosophy becomes: “Knowledge should never be a luxury.”
* Master Invisible Lifeline. She wants to develop her gift until she can reach people who would otherwise be impossible to save—while discovering how to use it without destroying herself in the process.
* Become a renowned healer—not for fame, but access. If becoming respected gives her the influence and resources necessary to help more people, she will pursue it.
* Return something to her hometown. Rather than simply escaping poverty, she wants to improve the community that raised her—perhaps establishing a school, clinic, communal garden, or combination of all three.
* Create her own medical text. She eventually wants to compile everything she has learned into a book containing practical medicine, magical healing, herbalism, gardening, and treatments that even ordinary families can understand.
* Protect her family’s future. She wants to reach the point where one bad harvest, illness, or financial hardship can never again threaten her family’s survival.
* Learn that Eymriella deserves a life too. This could be her most important personal goal—even though she wouldn’t recognize it as a goal at Level 1. She begins believing, “My purpose is taking care of everyone else.” Her character arc can slowly teach her, “I am allowed to have dreams that are mine.”

A particularly strong main quest goal for her would be: earn enough to build her parents their dream home. Her personal quest is uncovering the origin of Invisible Lifeline, and her hidden emotional goal is learning to accept care from others instead of always being the caretaker. Those three give her a reason to adventure, a mystery for the DM to develop, and room for character growth.
## Goals of Rafferty Rookwood

**Short-to-Mid-Term Goal: Recover the Gargoyle's Weapons**
Raff wants to help his gargoyle companion recover its missing weapons and possessions.

Their current adventure may provide useful information.

Immediate actions: Complete their current contract, collect payment, and seek information from their employer.

Success: Helping the gargoyle recover its missing possessions.

Potential challenges: Lost leads, competing interests, and obstacles that require the assistance of their wider adventuring party.

**Mid-to-Long-Term Goal: Investigate the Necromancer**
Raff wants to discover what the necromancer intended when he was poisoned and transformed into what he is.

He wants to understand why this happened, why he was involved, and whether the resulting transformation was intentional.

He is not necessarily seeking a cure, generally believing that it is impossible and that blind hope would hurt more than acceptance.

Success: Discovering the truth behind the transformation. Potential plot hooks.

Potential challenges: Finding reliable information, confronting his distrust of spellcasters, and dealing with discoveries that could change his understanding of himself.

This goal may become more important depending on what is revealed.

**Long-Term Goal: A New Lease on Life**
Raff wants to build a fulfilling new life and find people he can genuinely consider family.

Having lost his former life, he now values the relationships he builds through adventuring. His gargoyle companion is already becoming an important part of that new family.

A meaningful milestone would be developing lasting, reciprocal familial bonds with his companions. This is an aspiration, not an outcome I'm asking to guarantee.

Potential challenges: Conflicting loyalties, maintaining relationships through difficult decisions, and learning to accept that other people genuinely care about him despite his own perceived self-worth.
