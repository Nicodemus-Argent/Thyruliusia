Small Island nation off the eastern coast of the Nation of Silverwood.

Home of the Fox and Ferret peoples.
Only known home of the Sakura / Cherry Blossom Trees. The festival of their blooming is a highly coveted experience, and very few outsiders are ever invited to attend.

For Context: Homes and culture in the styles of the Koreans and Japanese.
