# The Seven Shields
The Seven Shields is a private military company founded by Seven Dwarves from Giledzar  [[Adventure 5e Thyruliusia/Nations/Deldruke Kingdom - Nation/Deldruke Kingdom - Nation\|Deldruke Kingdom - Nation]]. Wanting self determination from the huge scale military of their home land, they ventured to the neighboring country of Ladriande  [[Adventure 5e Thyruliusia/Nations/Ladriande - Nation/Ladriande - Nation\|Ladriande - Nation]], and set up shop in the capital.
