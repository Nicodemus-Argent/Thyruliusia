Note: While the other elves are regularly called 'Gray', 'Green', or 'Dark' by others, only these Elves call themselves "Sun" or "Golden", and others mock them. The most other people are willing to grant them is "High" Elves. But that is usually said sarcastically.

The Sun Elves are the only ones that are as tall as humans, 5 to 6.5 feet tall.

The Radiant Stone Cities of the Sun Elves

- **Culture & Architecture:** These elves are the builders of grand, lasting civilization. They erect soaring monuments, sweeping marble plazas, and massive stone walls designed to stand for millennia.
- **Map Placement & Resources:** They build on high, sun-drenched plateaus, fertile coastal cliffs, or strategic plains. They produce fine architectural stone, advanced magical items, refined metals, and historical arcana.

These Elves do not mine, but they buy large quantities of raw ore and perfect it in different ways than the Dwarves or Gnomes do. And they rarely sell these metals, let alone the products of them. They can refine metals for armor and weapons, but most famously refine the precious metals to purity no one else can and shape it with patience of a century.
