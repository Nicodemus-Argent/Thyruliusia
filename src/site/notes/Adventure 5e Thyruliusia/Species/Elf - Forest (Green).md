The Tree-Woven Canopy of the Green Elves

- **Culture & Magic:** Influenced by their horned _Magic: The Gathering_ aesthetics, these elves practice a slow, generational form of biomancy. They do not cut stone or lumber. Instead, they guide the growth of massive, braided trees over a century to create living fortresses in the clouds.
- **Map Placement & Resources:** They occupy the largest, most ancient primeval forests. Their primary resources are rare wood, medicinal forest herbs, alchemical sap, and trained beasts of burden.

These Elves do not mine under ground. They do happily import processed metals from others. But they do have a very secret craft of drawing precious metals out of the earth. They plant magically designed trees that grow deep, deep into the earth. They find the ore veins with their roots and pull the metal up into their trunk. The saplings grow tall, but thin. They are very hard with the metal ore integral to their structure. In very sacred ceremonies with alchemy reagents and a magic blade, these saplings are cut and then fashioned into weapons, tools, ornaments, magical implements, and furniture. Outsiders sometimes glimpse these beautiful trees of silver and steel, but never allowed to learn the intent of them.
**The Final Products:** Weapons forged from these saplings are lighter than steel but just as sharp, carrying a natural resonance for magic. A silver-wood staff or a steel-grained shield is a priceless heirloom.

## Appearance
They are the same 'race', 'species', and 'sub-species'; but there are two distinct kinds. The horned "Caprine" and the antlered "Cervine". The two are entirely the same. The children are born with unadorned heads and begin growing the horns / antlers around age 20. When they come from mixed heritage, they have whichever type their same gendered parent had.


## Features

### Caer Barle
**Its Modern Function:** Today, the fort serves as **The Warden's Academy**. It is a premier military training ground where horned caprine and antlered cervine elves learn advanced guerrilla warfare, archery, and stealth tactics, keeping the nation's defenses sharp despite the current era of peace.
